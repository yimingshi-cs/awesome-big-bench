# Edition-level sources and dates · 23 November 2026

Scope: publisher-identified changes to tasks, environments or grading. See the [version registry](../data/editions/) for the active rows and their primary sources. Formatted report labels survive unchanged. A provider run with a different harness is not automatically another benchmark edition; a release dossier that only mentions a family does not prove it used every edition.

## Terminal-Bench

The [publisher’s dated news index](https://www.tbench.ai/news) distinguishes the scored dataset releases from calls for contributions, individual challenge tasks, Harbor itself, science spin-offs and leaderboard-policy posts. The May 19, 2025 [original announcement](https://www.tbench.ai/news/announcement) names the launch set **Core-v0**; the [November 2025 announcement](https://www.tbench.ai/news/announcement-2-0) calls that same May launch **Terminal-Bench 1.0**. Reuse one stable entry at `terminal-bench` rather than fabricating a separate day for 1.0. The [legacy dataset registry](https://github.com/harbor-framework/terminal-bench-1/blob/main/registry.json) also pins Core 0.1.0 and a compatibility update 0.1.1; implementation and dataset version numbering must not be interchanged. The registry calls both launch tasks, and its task selectors encode some difficulty levels differently: do not infer additional leaderboard editions from the number of identifiers alone.

- [2.0](https://www.tbench.ai/news/announcement-2-0), 2025-11-07; [2.1](https://www.tbench.ai/news/terminal-bench-2-1), 2026-05-06. New suite then corrections to 28/89 tasks.
- [3.0](https://www.tbench.ai/news/terminal-bench-3-0), 2026-07-30; [4.0](https://www.tbench.ai/news/terminal-bench-4-0), 2026-08-28; [4.1](https://www.tbench.ai/news/terminal-bench-4-1), 2026-11-09. These are the authors’ public launch/announcement dates. GitHub’s GitHub Release publication timestamps for the same versions are [July 23](https://github.com/harbor-framework/terminal-bench/releases/tag/v3.0.0), [August 26](https://github.com/harbor-framework/terminal-bench/releases/tag/v4.0.0), and [November 3](https://github.com/harbor-framework/terminal-bench/releases/tag/v4.1.0), respectively. A tag and a public launch are different dated events; this is not a six-day contradiction. Distinct public posting dates do not prove when private repos or pre-release checkpoints became visible.
- 3.0 has 74 tasks; 4.0 removes eight and changes resource settings. 4.1 keeps 4.0’s agent-facing environment and task set but applies revised verifiers to saved outputs. It earns its own row on grading grounds. A CPU-only subset or a named agent runner is not another full suite version. The 4.1 announcement describes 5.0 as a milestone for future work, not a published benchmark.
- FrontierBench v0.1 in Claude Opus 5 is a development-phase result, not evidence of using the full final 3.0 task set. Keep the release report as such rather than projecting that alias onto a 3.0-edition score. Do not backfill 4.1 with mainline data for 4.0.

## τ family

Primary evidence: original Sierra [τ-bench](https://sierra.ai/resources/research/tau-bench), [τ² announcement](https://sierra.ai/blog/benchmarking-agents-in-collaborative-real-world-scenarios), [τ³ announcement](https://sierra.ai/blog/bench-advancing-agent-benchmarking-to-knowledge-and-voice), [published release tags](https://github.com/sierra-research/tau2-bench/releases) and [pinned maintainer changelog](https://github.com/sierra-research/tau2-bench/blob/v2.0.0/CHANGELOG.md).

- τ-bench (2024-06-17); τ²-bench (June 10, 2025 publisher announcement; [paper v1 June 9](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07982)); [τ² repository 0.1.3](https://github.com/sierra-research/tau2-bench/releases/tag/v0.1.3) (2025-08-26), which removes default natural-language assertion checks. A bare τ² result remains family-level if the patch is unstated.
- The 1.0.0 package corresponds to marketed τ³-bench (2026-03-18); these are one row. Knowledge retrieval and voice are different reported tasks/modes; do not infer a voice result from a text-domain result. [1.0.1](https://github.com/sierra-research/tau2-bench/releases/tag/v1.0.1) (2026-07-22 UTC published tag, changes on main July 14–15) corrected grading specifically for banking_knowledge. The other domains are unaffected—no blanket series-wide change to those scores.
- **τ-bench 2.0.0** is the October 2026 multilingual and voice suite, not the earlier τ²-bench. [Release notes](https://github.com/sierra-research/tau2-bench/releases/tag/v2.0.0) explicitly date it October 19; GitHub’s timestamp is October 20 at 00:28 UTC. It changes English items, judging, languages and simulator configurations, and the maintainers do not compare it directly with 1.x.
- 0.1.1/0.1.2 primarily change CLI/install. 0.2.0 introduces the web leaderboard. 0.2.1 records support for train/test splits, gym/interactive interfaces and optional protocol enforcement; we found no evidence that it replaces the standard graded benchmark. Do not turn an optional split, software-only operation, or every language/voice manifest into a major public edition by default. No first-party τ⁴ launch was located in these materials.

## ARC and APEX

- Original ARC/ARC-AGI-1: [2019-11-05 paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.01547); [ARC-AGI-2 public release 2025-03-24](https://arcprize.org/blog/announcing-arc-agi-2-and-arc-prize-2025). A change of evaluation harness is not another ARC suite, nor is AI2's separate ARC-Challenge an edition of this family.
- [ARC-AGI-3 preview 2025-07-17](https://arcprize.org/blog/arc-agi-3-preview-30-day-learnings) is supported by the maintainers' dated retrospective. Its [preview challenge archive](https://arcprize.org/competitions/arc-agi-3-preview-agents/archive) distinguishes the July 18 competition kickoff and defines the hidden-games ranking. Full [ARC-AGI-3 launched 2026-03-25](https://arcprize.org/blog/arc-agi-3-launch), one day after [paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24621). The competition window is not the full benchmark’s release date.
- [APEX-Agents original launch, 2026-01-21](https://www.mercor.com/blog/introducing-apex-agents/), one day after [the v1 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14242); [1.1 public release 2026-09-08](https://www.mercor.com/blog/introducing-apex-agents-1-1/). 1.1 refines 240 tasks and changes how the grader handles hedged, conflicting answers. Mercor’s “APEX-1” and APEX-SWE are sister benchmarks, not APEX-Agents versions.

## Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index

The publisher's [version history](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking#version-history) is the source of truth for methodology, not an indexed model's launch date. The fourteen documented entries in `aa.json` include one collective early period, v1.0–1.3; there is no evidence here for four individual dates or four separate scoring rules in that collective. v2.0 began 11 February 2025, v2.1 5 August, v2.2 6 August and v3.0 2 September, per the explicit start boundaries in that history. v4.0’s [AA announcement](https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys/status/2008570646897573931) is dated 6 January 2026. Contemporaneous AA articles date [4.1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-v4-1) to June 15, [4.1.1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-v4-1-1) to August 6, [4.2](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-v4-2) to September 4, [4.3](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-v4-3) to September 7, and [5.0](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-v5) to October 27. Different weighting, task sets, anchor models and judge panels change score comparability even without a new index name.

Do not render an interval bound as a launch date. For the original period the issuer only gives January 2024 (Jan 31 is a mathematical upper bound). For v4.3.1 and v4.3.2 the official start granularity is September 2026; September 29 is a conservative available-by bound because an [AA article](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gpt-6-1-sol-replaces-gpt-6-sol-after-just-7-days-with-near-astra-intelligence) already reports v4.3.2 (the v4.3.1 step is earlier in the issuer's chronology). For v5.0.1 the official start granularity is November 2026; [AA explicitly names it on November 5](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/thinking-machines-releases-inkling-2-small-highest-scoring-open-weights-model-outside-of-chinia-upon-weights-release-it). Use month displays or explicitly identified upper bounds. Keep v5.0.1 separate because the publisher actually regraded a component, not because it ends in .1. AI Progress Index and Coding Agent Index are independent indices, not editions.

## OSWorld, SWE-bench and LiveCodeBench

Sources and exact dates for each row are in `extra.json`. [OSWorld-Verified](https://xlang.ai/blog/osworld-verified) is a named 2025 benchmark revision, not every independently verified model submission. [The v2 maintainers' release ledger](https://github.com/xlang-ai/OSWorld-V2#-updates) says 26 June 2026 for launch; the first pinned manifest’s 2026.06.24 identifier is not an announcement date. [August 8](https://github.com/xlang-ai/OSWorld-V2/releases/tag/v2026.08.08) materially changes task code and verification; [September 16](https://github.com/xlang-ai/OSWorld-V2/releases/tag/osworld-v2.1) is 2.1. A hosting-only September 8 update is not another edition. Bare OSWorld 2.0 results may not identify the specific frozen snapshot.

The original, Lite, Verified, Multilingual, and Multimodal refer to different Princeton-led tasks or selected sets; retain the [original author’s variant definitions](https://www.swebench.com/). Multilingual does not mean translated issue text. The [original 517-task Multimodal suite and 480-task V2](https://www.swebench.com/multimodal.html) use different selection/evaluation protocols. Scale’s [Pro](https://scale.com/blog/swe-bench-pro) and [Pro V2](https://labs.scale.com/blog/swe-bench-pro-v2) are independently created, separate benchmarks in the repository-engineering lineage. A new scaffold, model, ranking tab, fixed timeout for a single run, or commercial/private split alone is not a new public suite edition. The historical public data commits for Lite and Multilingual set exact, linkable availability evidence; they do not make an empty repo’s creation time a launch.

The [LiveCodeBench maintainers](https://github.com/LiveCodeBench/LiveCodeBench#dataset-versions) enumerate six named cumulative code-generation releases. Their task windows end in **March, May, July and September 2024, January and April 2025**. Those are *problem coverage* dates. First substantive versioned HF commits cited for v2–v6 are June 2, August 12, October 6, January 14 and April 21, respectively, in UTC. v1’s March 12, 2024 public paper and data commit agree on the day. Do not turn date-range slices, flags, agent configurations or a separate LiveCodeBench Pro into “v7”. The general paper also covers code execution, self-repair and test-output prediction; the six cumulative problem counts above specifically describe the code-generation snapshots.
