# OpenAI historical general and reasoning releases

Evidence through **22 November 2026 (Singapore)**. This file complements rather than repeats the separately audited GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6.2 Astra/Sol reports. An entry is a **release × benchmark label × source** receipt, not a score or a count of independent new benchmarks. A row may be a separately reported track, setting, language or context interval. An announcement and a technical card may both supply evidence. The release date identifies the model/checkpoint; where the technical report was published later, its date is not retroactively assigned to the model.

## What can be used immediately

- **619 sourced rows; 28 releases, snapshots or preview/default updates with qualifying results.** A new row is not a new model generation. Use the exact `benchmark` field and source location for display; `family` is a comparison key, not a claim that different editions have interchangeable scores.
- **HumanEval:** the [GPT-4 technical report](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.08774v1#page=7) has a GPT-4 result in Table 2. The [original OpenAI simple-evals release](https://github.com/openai/simple-evals/blob/294cb1fb18f7aed4e21dc567350b0761a9e6f699/README.md) describes both GPT-4o and the April 2024 GPT-4 Turbo snapshot as targets. Do not count HumanEval automatically for a model that merely trains a different student model on it inside PostTrainBench.
- **Toolathlon:** explicit target cells in the [GPT-5.2](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-2/), [5.4](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-4/), [5.5](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/) and [5.6 Sol](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/) full-size release tables. ToolBench and ToolEval are distinct names.
- **APEX-Agents:** the [GPT-5.4 release](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-4/) quotes **Mercor**, the benchmark owner, explicitly identifying GPT-5.4 at the top of the APEX-Agents leaderboard. This is a publisher-reported result in a vendor release, not an OpenAI lab-run table row. “APEX” describes [Mercor’s family](https://www.mercor.com/apex/); no result is imputed to a sister suite.
- Positive evidence in this ledger is **not** available for AgentDojo, MMBench, LongBench or ToolBench solely because a neighboring name appears. MMMU is not MMBench, long-context tasks are not LongBench, and τ-bench/Toolathlon are not ToolBench. Absence here is not proof that no third-party organization has ever evaluated a model on them.

## Additional coverage boundaries

- **Later interval:** GPT-6.1 Sol (29 Sep) and GPT-6.2 Astra/Sol (17 Nov) belong in the existing two-release inventory, not here. GPT-6 in ChatGPT (7 Oct) was checked independently. The [29 October release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9624314-model-release-notes) expressly say the Plus/Pro main GPT-6 model was unchanged. The 26 Oct GPT-6.1 Terra release was an efficient tier, not a separate flagship. GPT-6 Rosalind, GPT-6 Cyber, CSAM classification and GPT-Live-1.1 were specialized, classifier or realtime tracks. The [API changelog](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog) was checked through the 19 Nov realtime update. No extra flagship inferred through the requested cutoff.
- **Excluded lines:** smaller-only o3-mini and o4-mini, GPT-4o mini, 4.1 mini/nano, GPT-5.x mini/nano, specialized Codex, deep-research agents, GPT Image/audio/realtime, open-weight gpt-oss and other specialist systems. A co-released full-size model can qualify without importing the smaller model’s results. ChatGPT product launches are not all a new model generation.
- **Measurement policy:** no conversion of a post-training student target, predecessor baseline, anonymized demo, pretraining corpus, cited paper, or benchmark mentioned only as motivation into focal release evidence. In particular, OpenAI’s internal GDPval v2 must not be conflated with an earlier public GDPval or the distinct Artificial Analysis GDPval-AA v2. Internal model-development versions, stress cases and closed variants are omitted even when their table labels are visible. Withheld benchmark items alone do not invalidate a documented public protocol.
- **Limits:** this is a broad release-source inventory, not a claim of exhaustive full-history harvesting. Secondary reviews, benchmark-site popularity, and third-party leaderboard rows not expressly attributable to a qualifying release are not used to fill gaps. Some original video-only o3 preview detail, final safety/partner-card appendices, silent snapshot updates and original card figures without a defensible named protocol remain unlisted. Live cards mutate: release dates must not be mistaken for publication dates of every later card revision. Bibliographic names only are recorded for dual-use capability tests; no task instances are provided.
