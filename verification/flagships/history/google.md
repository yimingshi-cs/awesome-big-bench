# Gemini historical flagship and reasoning evaluations

**279 release × evaluation records, 17 releases or named updates, through the last pre-Argon flagship.** [benchmarks.csv](../../../data/flagship_history.csv) identifies the exact target, release date, benchmark and location in the first-party source. [catalog_matches.csv](google-catalog.csv) is a separate 96-record crosswalk to **28** entries in the 104-entry November 2026 catalog. There are no scores in either file.

The date belongs to the named release, not necessarily to the technical report. Gemini 1.0 Pro is included alongside Ultra because it was the initial jointly launched general model and was separately evaluated in the same report; Nano is not. A named reasoning-mode release is eligible even before the “Deep Think” brand. Ordinary Flash/Lite, media/audio/robotics products and competition-specific research systems are not treated as general-purpose flagships. A reference, training-monitoring description, baseline-column score, product illustration or internal-only benchmark is not release-level usage evidence.

## Identity and scope checks

* The 104-entry website crosswalk uses exact names or an identifiable public protocol/subset: GPQA Diamond→GPQA, BIRD-SQL dev→BIRD, named METR RE-Bench subset→RE-Bench, named FutureHouse LAB-Bench subsets→LAB-Bench, and Terminal-Bench 2.0→Terminal-Bench with its version retained. **MMMU-Pro does not establish MMMU; LiveCodeBench Pro is not collapsed into LiveCodeBench; τ²-bench is not τ-bench; GDPval-AA is not the original GDPval; ARC-AGI-2 is not ARC-AGI or ARC-AGI-3.** SWE-bench Verified maps to its own registry entry, not the unsuffixed SWE-bench.
* Literal keyword matches are unsafe: “Bird (2020)” in the Kalamang paper is not BIRD; “Pope” in author lists is not POPE; “Gemini Apps” is not APPS. The positive BIRD evidence is the 2.0 Pro results graphic. Similarly, measuring a low-needle variant does not establish RULER or LongBench.
* **Excluded on purpose:** Real Toxicity Prompts / Winogender / WinoBias where only training monitoring or unversioned method prose is given; BBQ retained only with downstream 1.5 Pro Fig. 26 evidence. HarmBench is merely cited by the 1.5 report; JailbreakBench is actually applied and measured. The 3 Pro FACTS table explicitly labels a held-out *internal* suite; this particular score is neither a public FACTS Grounding result nor sufficient by itself for a public suite usage row. The internal Google RE-Bench used in much later models is **not** automatically METR RE-Bench. Publicly defined datasets with held-out items are not the same thing as benchmarks explicitly designated internal.
* Comparative columns from other-model releases are not backdated. Model cards sometimes used earlier precursor checkpoints for safety testing: for example, 3 Pro FSF Figs. 5–6 explicitly identify a “similar earlier version”; the crosswalk does not use those charts to establish a final-model stealth result. The 3.1 Pro high-compute safety rows explicitly marked “Deep Think mode” are not evidence for the standard Pro column.
* Coverage is broad, not every Google experiment: no assertion that every transient AI Studio build, Bard/app configuration, undisclosed safety workload, per-language split or one-off demo received a public benchmark release. Anonymous Arena labels are not mapped back to named checkpoints by inference. Several release tables are images; the relevant score-bearing panels were inspected together with their model-labelled columns. Locations refer to printed table/section labels in the linked edition, not mutable aggregator cards.
