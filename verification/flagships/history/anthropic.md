# Claude historical evaluation inventory

Cutoff: **22 November 2026, Singapore time.** The compact CSV has **674 release–benchmark rows**, **142 working family labels**, and **20 releases with qualifying evidence**. This is Anthropic-specific release evidence, not a census of all external papers that have tested Claude. The March 2023 launch and its May context expansion are documented even though they contribute zero rows.

## What was reviewed

The release ledger includes broadly capable flagship and reasoning-model streams, not just the highest price tier. Separate Opus and Sonnet launches stay distinct; a same-day joint family announcement is one release. That is why 3.5/3.7 Sonnet, the later independent Sonnet reasoning models, and September’s 5.5 Opus/Sonnet launches are included. The restricted general-purpose Mythos Preview is explicitly marked. Instant, Haiku, specialty-only deployments and tool feature launches are not separate entries. Fable/Mythos 5.1 and 5.2 are already covered by the focal comparison; they are intentionally not rerun or double-counted here.

A row requires an attributed test of the release, with an identifiable public benchmark, public owner/project, or publicly documented evaluation method. The row links a dated official launch/technical note or a specific PDF page in the cited card. When card sections and partner reports overlap, the compact row provides a representative evidence location rather than every duplicate figure. Subsets and separately named editions are retained where material. Explicitly internal test sets and private replacement problems are excluded; running the public tasks under an internal **harness** is not disqualifying. One family label is not a guarantee of comparable grading. No scores or operational safety-test details are reproduced.

These are measurements with Anthropic’s runner, **not** certified official-harness scores. The later Verified entries explicitly identify the authors’ June 2026 task release, with frozen task prompts, ground truth and execution checks. Earlier tables report Toolathlon against the published tasks; they are not retroactively renamed Verified. Fable 5.1’s separately audited Verified row belongs to the focal-list erratum, outside these six.

**APEX / APEX-Agents:** no positively attributed focal result under this name was found in the historical Anthropic launch text, card/appendix text or reviewed launch comparison graphics. No Anthropic history row was manufactured from a third-party leaderboard, another vendor, or a nearby name. This is not a claim that nobody ever tested Claude on APEX.

Public/near-name distinctions include external FinanceAgent versus Anthropic Real-World Finance; Harvey BigLaw Bench versus Harvey Legal Agent Benchmark; MMLU versus MMMLU/GMMLU; Cybench versus CyberGym and ExploitGym; and QA datasets used as material in an alignment experiment versus direct QA accuracy. For the latter, the family is **CoT-Control**, not GPQA/MMLU-Pro/HLE. FrontierBench v0.1 in the Opus 5 card refers to the terminal-agent benchmark; do not use that name alone to merge unrelated projects.

## Coverage limits

We inspected the launch texts, comparison figures/methodology notes, available official cards/addenda and their appendices, including text-layer and figure-caption searches plus targeted reading of the adjoining claims. Tabular/caption results were attributed to the focal model; a citation list, predecessor column, saturated/retired benchmark or redacted blocklist was not treated as a new release result. Card pages can include claims about multiple models; use the focal row/paragraph. The current Claude 3 PDF includes later addenda, which are separately reviewed only for their own releases.

This is a **verified named-evaluation inventory, not a claim of exhaustive private or figure-level coverage**. No complete public audit of unpublished partner suites, individual unlabelled figures, every gated problem set, all private safety tests or unreported scores is available. Informal demos, bespoke tests with no identifiable public definition and internal-only benchmark versions are not silently counted as standard public tasks. The 20 inspected card/appendix documents cover 2705 PDF pages (including repeated background/appended material). Composite index components and every ablation/effort point were not exploded into separate benchmarks. No automated scan alone establishes a universal absence claim.
